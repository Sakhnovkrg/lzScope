lzScope

A free stereo oscilloscope plugin in the CLAP format for Windows, macOS and Linux. Small, fast, written entirely in Free Pascal.

Download Source on GitHub

Features

  • Stereo waveformLeft and right channels overlaid, or split into two lanes.
  • Sync to the hostThe view locks to the song's bars, from 1/4 bar to 8 bars, with a playhead.
  • Deep zoomFrom 5 seconds down to single samples; level range from -60 to +36 dB.
  • Trigger modesInstant, any, ascending or descending zero crossing.
  • Hold and scrubFreeze the picture, drag back through 20 seconds of history, zoom under the cursor.
  • Crosshair readoutLevel, time, frequency and per-channel peak at the cursor.
  • Your defaultsSave the current settings as the starting point for new instances.
  • LightweightA single file of about 200 KB, GPU-drawn, sharp at any display scale.

Download and install

Windows

7 or later, 64-bit.
Copy lzscope.clap to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP

Download for Windows

macOS

10.13 or later on Intel, 11 or later on Apple Silicon.
Copy lzScope.clap to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP

Download for macOS

Linux

64-bit x86, glibc 2.34 or later, X11 or XWayland.
Copy lzscope.clap to ~/.clap

Download for Linux
  1. Download the zip for your system and unpack it.
  2. Copy the plugin into the CLAP folder shown above; create the folder if it is not there.
  3. Restart your DAW or rescan plugins. lzScope is listed under the vendor DsgDnB.

Works in any CLAP host: REAPER, Bitwig Studio, FL Studio and others. Needs OpenGL.
